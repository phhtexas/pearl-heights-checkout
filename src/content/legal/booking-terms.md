These Terms explain how a direct reservation is formed, paid, verified, changed and canceled. The checkout displays the exact property, dates, guest count, charges, payment schedule and date-specific cancellation amounts before payment.

A reservation made through Airbnb, Vrbo or another marketplace is governed by that marketplace's booking terms and the listing rules shown there. These direct-booking Terms do not modify a marketplace reservation.

## 2 Eligibility and responsible guest

The reservation holder must be at least 25 years old, provide accurate information, complete required verification, sign the Rental Agreement and remain at the property throughout the stay. The reservation holder is responsible for registered occupants, approved visitors and compliance with the booking documents.

Occupancy may not exceed the limit displayed for the property. Only registered occupants may stay overnight. Visitors, pets, events and exceptions require prior written approval when the listing or Rental Agreement requires it.

## 3 Price quote and total

The quote identifies the nightly charges, cleaning charge, included $79 damage-waiver charge, taxes, discounts and final total. The damage waiver provides up to $5,000 in coverage, subject to the provider terms, exclusions, reporting requirements and claim decisions presented at checkout. Mandatory charges known and calculable in advance are included in the displayed lodging total; government taxes may be itemized separately. The final amount to be paid is displayed before you authorize payment.

A quote is based on the selected property, dates, occupancy and information available when generated. It is not a reservation hold unless checkout displays an active price-lock period. When checkout displays a 15-minute price lock, the quoted price is held only until that timer expires; availability is not guaranteed until payment succeeds and we issue a confirmation.

## 4 How a reservation is formed

You submit a booking request by entering the required information, selecting a payment option, accepting the linked policies and authorizing payment. A reservation becomes confirmed when the initial payment succeeds, we create the reservation and we send a confirmation. We may reject or reverse a booking before confirmation if dates are unavailable, payment is unauthorized, material information is incorrect or the booking creates a safety, fraud or legal concern.

Payment confirmation does not release the exact street address or access code. Those items remain conditional on the signed Rental Agreement and completed identity verification.

## 5 Payment options

### Pay in full

Pay in full is the default option. If check-in is within 30 days, the entire amount is due at booking.

### Optional two-part payment when check-in is more than 30 days away

Pay in full remains the default. When check-in is more than 30 days away, checkout may also offer an optional two-part payment: 50 percent at booking and the remaining 50 percent automatically charged at 4:00 p.m. Central Time 30 days before check-in. Checkout shows both amounts and charge dates before payment.

By completing the booking, you authorize us and Stripe to store the payment method and charge the scheduled balance without a second checkout. You are responsible for keeping the payment method valid and for notifying us promptly if it changes.

## 6 Failed scheduled payment

If the scheduled second payment fails, the 72-hour cure period begins at the first decline. We will send a notice promptly stating the reason available to us and the deadline to resolve it. You must contact support before that deadline, and we will send a secure Stripe link for updating the payment method or completing payment. We will not ask you to send complete card information by email, text or portal message.

A reservation is not automatically canceled at the first decline. If the balance remains unpaid after the stated 72-hour deadline, Pearl Heights Homes may cancel the reservation manually. Any refund is then determined under the Cancellation Policy based on the cancellation date, amounts paid and applicable exceptions.

The checkout and calculation engine must apply the disclosed provider rule consistently.

### No-show and early departure

A no-show or voluntary early departure is treated as a cancellation at or after 12:00 a.m. Central Time on the check-in date. Unused nights are not refunded unless required by law or approved in writing.
