## 4 Identity verification and biometric information

The reservation holder completes identity verification through Stripe Identity. Depending on the configured method, Stripe may capture a government ID and a selfie and may create biometric identifiers or face-geometry information to confirm that the document belongs to the person presenting it. Stripe presents its own notice and consent flow before capture.

Our application database stores the verification outcome, timestamps and provider references, not copies of the ID or selfie. Stripe may store the underlying images, extracted ID information, device information and verification data on our behalf and may make those records available to a small number of authorized Stripe account administrators. We restrict access and do not routinely download those materials.

We instruct Stripe to redact personal verification-session data 120 days after checkout or cancellation unless a documented legal, fraud, insurance or dispute hold requires longer retention. Stripe may separately retain information as an independent controller under its privacy notice. Stripe states that biometric identifiers are retained for no longer than one year and that other submitted identity information may be retained longer for its disclosed legal, fraud-prevention and service purposes.

If you do not consent to selfie-based verification, contact us before completing the verification. We will consider a reasonably equivalent non-biometric method consistent with property safety, fraud prevention and applicable law. Declining biometric processing does not waive the requirement to verify the responsible guest.

We do not sell, lease or use biometric identifiers for marketing. We use verification data only to verify identity, prevent fraud or misuse, protect safety, administer the reservation and comply with law. You may contact us to request deletion; when appropriate, we will redact the Stripe verification session. You may also contact Stripe about information it controls independently.

## 5 Payments

Stripe processes direct-booking payments and stores payment credentials used for an authorized later installment or permitted reservation charge. Pearl Heights Homes receives transaction identifiers, limited payment-method details and payment status but does not receive or store the complete card number in its application database. Stripe's own terms and privacy notice apply to its processing.

## 12 Privacy rights and requests

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or a portable copy of personal data and to opt out of targeted advertising, certain sales or sharing, and qualifying profiling. You may also withdraw marketing consent. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

Submit a request to bookings@pearlheightshomes.com with the subject "Privacy Request." We will authenticate the request using information reasonably related to your interaction with us. Do not email a copy of your government ID unless we specifically provide a secure method. Where applicable, we respond within 45 days and will explain any permitted extension or denial.

If we deny a request, you may appeal by emailing bookings@pearlheightshomes.com with the subject "Privacy Appeal" and explaining the basis for the appeal. We will review the appeal and provide instructions for contacting the Texas Attorney General or another regulator when required by law. Authorized agents may submit requests where legally permitted, subject to verification of authority and identity.
