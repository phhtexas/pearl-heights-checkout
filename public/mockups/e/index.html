Your questions, answered

You get the same homes and the same Superhost care, with the hosts one message away. Your full total — taxes, damage waiver and all — is shown before you pay, and questions go straight to Oscar and Fernanda, who usually reply within an hour.

You'll get a confirmation email. Then you sign the rental agreement and verify your ID online (about 3 minutes each). The address, entry steps and WiFi appear in your private stay page once both are done; door codes arrive the morning of check-in day.

Check-in is after 4:00 PM and check-out is before 10:00 AM at every home, with self check-in.

Booked more than 7 days before check-in: cancel within 24 hours after booking for everything back, no fee. After that: 30 days or more before check-in, everything back minus a 5% processing fee; 14 to 29 days before, 50% of the stay plus the damage waiver; less than 14 days before, the damage waiver only (until the day before check-in). Booked 7 days or less before check-in: paid in full and non-refundable. Your exact dates and amounts are shown before you pay.

Yes — every booking includes a damage waiver with $5,000 of coverage for accidental damage during your stay, subject to the provider's terms. It isn't a security deposit: nothing is held on your card.
